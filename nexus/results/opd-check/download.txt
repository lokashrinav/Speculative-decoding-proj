# Qwen3-4B-Thinking-Draft-OPD

This repository contains **Qwen3-4B-Thinking-Draft-OPD**, a draft model for speculative decoding introduced in the paper [Draft-OPD: On-Policy Distillation for Speculative Draft Models](https://huggingface.co/papers/2605.29343).

- **Project Page:** [https://www.haodilei.top/draft-opd/](https://www.haodilei.top/draft-opd/)
- **GitHub Repository:** [https://github.com/bingyang-lei/Draft-OPD](https://github.com/bingyang-lei/Draft-OPD)

## Model Details

- **Target Model:** [`Qwen3-4B(enable_thinking=True)`](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-4B)
- **Model type:** Draft model for speculative decoding
- **Architecture:** Same as the original DFlash draft model
- **Post-training method:** Draft-OPD (On-Policy Distillation)

## Performance and Training Method

Draft-OPD trains speculative draft models with on-policy target feedback. Instead of only learning from fixed target-generated trajectories, the drafter is supervised on draft-induced states exposed during speculative verification, including the positions where draft proposals are rejected. 

Experiments show that Draft-OPD achieves over $5\times$ lossless acceleration for thinking models across diverse tasks, improving over EAGLE-3 and DFlash by 23% and 13%. For detailed training procedures, evaluation settings, and performance results, please refer to our paper: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29343](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29343).
